How Ration कार्ड कट ह या नही और कितना Ration मिलता ह चक करे BPL Works

State Food & Civil Supplies Department list update karta hai, online portal par publish karta hai, aur Fair Price Shops per ration distribute karta hai. Beneficiaries ko monthly card ke through entitlement milta hai, aur any discrepancy ko grievance redressal system handle karta hai.

Q1Mera Ration Card cut ho gaya hai, phir bhi BPL list mein dikhta hai?
A
Agar card cut ho gaya hai to aapko turant nearest Fair Price Shop ya Ration Office mein jaake re‑activation ke liye apply karna chahiye, warna aapko ration milne ka adhikar nahi milega.
Q2BPL list ka latest version kaise download karun?
A
State govt ki official food supply website ya mPin app par login karein, 'BPL List Download' section mein jaake PDF ya Excel format mein latest list download kar sakte hain.
Q3Agar mera address change ho gaya hai to kya karna hai?
A
Aapko apne Ration Office mein address proof (electricity bill, rent agreement) ke saath update request deni hogi, aur portal par bhi new address update karna na bhoolen.
