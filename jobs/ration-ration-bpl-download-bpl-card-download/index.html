State food corporation ya PDS department online portal par BPL list upload karta hai. Beneficiaries apna ration card number dal kar status dekhte hain, aur PDF download kar ke apne local fair price shop mein present karte hain for ration collection.

Q1Mera ration card BPL list mein nahi dikh raha, kya karun?
A
Sabse pehle apna Aadhaar number aur income proof verify karein. Agar data mismatch ho, to nearest PDS office mein grievance register karein aur updated documents submit karein.
Q2BPL card download karne ke liye kaunse documents chahiye?
A
Aadhaar card, latest ration card copy, income certificate (if required), aur recent utility bill (electricity/water) ka proof.
Q3BPL status verify karne ke baad ration kaise collect karun?
A
Download ki hui BPL certificate ko apne local fair price shop (FPS) mein le jaayein. Shop staff aapke card ko verify karega aur subsidised ration allocate karega.
