Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Overview

Haryana government ne Power Spray Pump Yojana launch ki hai jisme har eligible farmer ko spray pump machine par 3000 rupaye ki subsidy milti hai. Application ki last date 15 Jan 2026 hai, isliye jaldi se apply karein.

Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Expert Analysis

Yeh scheme small and marginal farmers ko modern irrigation tools adopt karne mein financial support deti hai, jo crop yield aur pest control dono ko improve karti hai. Subsidy se initial cost kam hoti hai, isliye adoption rate badhne ki sambhavna hai.

Who Is Eligible: Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स्

Scheme un sabhi kisanon ke liye hai jo Haryana mein registered landholders hain aur spray pump ka upyog karna chahte hain, chahe wo small ya marginal farmer ho.

Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Application Process

1. Apna land record (Patta) aur Kisan Credit Card ready rakhein. 2. Spray pump ki invoice ya quotation collect karein. 3. Online portal ya nearest Krishi Vibhag office se application form download karke sahi se fill karein.

Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Benefits Details

Har eligible farmer ko 3000 Rs ki direct subsidy milti hai, jo pump ki total cost ka 10-15% ho sakti hai. Yeh amount seedhi bank account ya cash voucher ke roop mein milti hai, jo purchase cost ko significantly kam karti hai.

How Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Works

Scheme ke tahat farmer apna application online ya offline submit karta hai, verification ke baad subsidy amount bank transfer ya voucher ke through release hoti hai. Implementation ko Krishi Vibhag monitor karta hai aur quarterly reports submit karta hai.

Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Application Tips

Application form mein sabhi details accurately fill karein, missing documents se rejection ho sakta hai. Original land records aur pump invoice attach karein, aur deadline se pehle submit karein taaki processing time kam ho.

Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् FAQs

Q1Is subsidy sirf ek baar milti hai ya repeatable hai?
A
Yeh subsidy ek hi spray pump purchase ke liye ek baar hi milti hai. Agla purchase ke liye alag se scheme apply karni padegi.
Q2Kya subsidy bank transfer ya cash voucher ke through milti hai?
A
Beneficiary ke bank account me direct transfer hoti hai, lekin kuch districts cash voucher bhi issue karte hain.
Q3Agar land record mismatch ho to kya karein?
A
Land record ko update karne ke liye nearest Patwari ya Tehsil office se contact karein, phir updated document ke saath application dubara submit karein.
