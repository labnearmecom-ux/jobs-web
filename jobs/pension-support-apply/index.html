Q1Pension ke liye kaun kaun se documents chahiye?
A
Aadhaar card, death certificate of mother, birth certificate of child, bank account details aur filled application form.
Q2Pension kitni der me milti hai?
A
Application approve hone ke baad, usually 30-45 din me bank account me monthly credit ho jati hai.
Q3Kya pension ko kisi aur purpose ke liye divert kar sakte hain?
A
Nahi, pension sirf eligible child ke welfare ke liye hi use karni hoti hai, misuse par penalty ho sakti hai.
